Event log — full audit trail

Every week opens with the pillow bank carried in, then lists every rupee that moved in the order it moved: the surplus draw, each instalment due, paid, received or missed (loan by loan), arrears cleared, loans taken and given. Each money row shows the change and the balance after it. The week closes with the loan book, the rating move, and a check that opening balance plus all movements equals the closing balance.
