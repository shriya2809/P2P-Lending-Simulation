Origination and unmet demand

A request is made only by someone who needed to borrow and was eligible to, so went to the market: it was either funded or found no lender. The last line counts people who needed to borrow but whom the rules did not allow to — rating below the exclusion rating, already holding the most loans their rating allows, or already at the borrowing ceiling. They cannot make a request, so they are not counted as requests. Every week, the people at or below the borrowing line are the funded, the unfunded and this last line added together.
